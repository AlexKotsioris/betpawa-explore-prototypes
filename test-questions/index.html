What the feature does (for the moderator)

On a multibet (combo), the user can secure one leg early. That leg locks in at an updated Potential Payout and can no longer let the multi down, whatever happens next. The rest of the multi keeps running — the user is paid if the remaining legs win, or if they cash the bet out. The only difference between the two versions is the wording of the button: “Early Settle Leg” vs “Cashout Leg.”

Moderator script

“Thanks for helping out. This is an early prototype, so some things won’t be clickable — that’s fine. There are no right or wrong answers; I’m testing the design, not you. Please think aloud as you go — tell me what you see, what you’d tap, and what you expect to happen.”

  1. Assign the participant one version (A or B). Split participants evenly across the two.
  2. Open that version on a phone and hand it over. Let them explore the bet in My Bets → Pending for a moment.
  3. Point them to the leg with the Early Settle Leg / Cashout Leg button. Ask: “Before tapping anything — what do you think this button does?” (This maps to Q4.)
  4. Let them tap through the flow and read the explanation. Then ask the comprehension questions below.
  5. Record which option they pick and their wording. The trap answers are what we’re watching for.

Comprehension questions

Q1 · Scope of the action
You’re on a 4-leg multi and you tap “Early Settle Leg” / “Cashout Leg” on one leg. What happens to the rest of your bet?
  • A The other three legs keep running as a live multi — the bet is still on.
  • B The whole bet is cashed out and settled right now.
  • C The other legs are cancelled and only this leg can pay.
  • D Not sure.

Watching for: do they think the whole bet ends? (Trap B — expected to be higher on “Cashout Leg”.)

Q2 · The secured leg
After you secure that leg, its selection goes on to lose later in the match. What happens to your multi?
  • A Nothing — that leg is locked in and can’t let the multi down.
  • B The whole multi loses, because one leg lost.
  • C Only that leg’s share of the stake is deducted.
  • D Not sure.

Watching for: do they grasp “set in stone… can no longer let your multi down”?

Q3 · Getting paid
You secured one leg, and your remaining legs all win. How are you paid?
  • A You win the multi at the updated Potential Payout shown after securing the leg.
  • B The secured leg is paid separately, as its own single bet.
  • C You only get the amount that was shown at the moment you secured the leg.
  • D Not sure.

Watching for: confusion between “secured a leg” and “cashed out the bet”.

Q4 · Wording (the key comparison)
Before reading any explanation — from the button wording alone, what did you expect it to do?
Ask this identically on both versions and compare the split.
  • A Secure just this one leg and keep the rest of the bet running.
  • B Cash out / end the whole bet now.
  • C Change or swap my pick on that leg.
  • D Not sure.

The headline metric: % choosing trap B on “Cashout Leg” vs “Early Settle Leg”. A big gap = the word “Cashout” is over-promising a whole-bet action.
